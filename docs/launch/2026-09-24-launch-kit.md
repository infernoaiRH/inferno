## 3. Long announcement (Telegram, Discord, blog)

**Inferno is live: AI chat on GPUs people lend, paid on Robinhood Chain**

Most AI runs in a few giant data centers. Meanwhile millions of good GPUs sit idle in gaming PCs and laptops for most of the day. Inferno connects the two.

**Chat.** Open Inferno and ask anything.
- *Private mode* downloads an open model once and runs it on your own GPU. Nothing you type leaves your browser tab, and it costs nothing.
- *Network mode* lets you pick someone lending a GPU, from the list or by pasting their wallet address. Your prompt is sealed in your browser to that lender's node key, and the answer comes back sealed. The Inferno relay only ever sees ciphertext. Each chat uses a fresh throwaway key, so a lender never learns your wallet.
- Every answer shows a receipt: the model, where it ran, how many tokens, how fast, and what it cost.

**Lend.** Put an idle GPU to work from a browser tab.
1. Test your GPU on the Lend page. It measures your card in a couple of seconds and estimates what it could serve.
2. Connect your wallet. Your address is how people find you and where you get paid.
3. Load a model and go online. Your wallet signs a free message linking your node to your address.

You keep 70% of what every answer you serve costs. What you earn depends on how many people use your node, how fast your GPU is, and how long you stay online. There's no guaranteed amount.

## Why no "easy money"

The brief asked for "earn and get easy money". The kit says "earn for every token" and "no guaranteed amount" instead, for three reasons:
- Regulators treat "easy money" crypto promotion as misleading financial promotion.
- It would contradict the terms above, and a contradiction is exactly what an unhappy buyer quotes back.
- It's the pitch the predecessor token used before it fell 98.8% (see `docs/research/opencomm-evaluation.md` §3).

"Your idle GPU can earn while you sleep" is both honest and exciting. Use that.
